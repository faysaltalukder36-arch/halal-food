    Privacy Policy

    This website collects information you submit through the order form, such as your name, phone number, address, quantity, and optional notes, so that we can process and confirm your order.

    How order information is used

    Submitted order information is used for order handling, delivery coordination, and customer communication. The order form is connected to a Google Apps Script and Google Sheet workflow.

    Direct contact

    If you contact us by phone or message, the information you provide may be used to respond to your request or process an order.

    Third-party services

    This website uses GitHub Pages for hosting and a Google Apps Script endpoint for order submission. Third-party services may process technical or submitted information according to their own policies.

    Questions

    For privacy questions, contact us using the phone number or contact options on this website.
