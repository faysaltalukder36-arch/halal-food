    Delivery & Returns

    Delivery areas

    We currently target all six districts of Barishal Division: Barishal, Patuakhali, Bhola, Pirojpur, Jhalokathi, and Barguna.

    Order confirmation

    After you submit an order, we confirm product availability and delivery details. Delivery timing and charges may depend on the destination and order.

    Product issues

    If there is a problem with an order, contact us as soon as possible with the order details. We will review the issue and discuss the appropriate solution.

    Before ordering

    For fresh meat and other products where stock or packaging can change, please confirm the current details with us before finalizing the order.
