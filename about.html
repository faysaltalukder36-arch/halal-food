    About Halal Food

    Halal Food is an online food and grocery storefront focused on simple product discovery, direct ordering, and home delivery.

    What we offer

    Our catalogue includes dates, dry food, honey, mix food, nuts and seeds, special masala, tea, and fresh meat.

    Our approach to halal information

    We do not claim independent halal certification on this website. If you need specific sourcing or supplier information for a product, please contact us before ordering.

    Ordering

    You can place an order through the online form or contact us directly. Current stock, packaging, and delivery details can be confirmed before an order is finalized.
